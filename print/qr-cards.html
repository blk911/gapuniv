Printing Instructions

  • Print on US Letter (8.5" x 11") paper
  • Use a good quality printer for best QR scanning
  • Print at 100% scale (do not fit to page)
  • Cut along the dashed lines
  • For bulk printing (~1,000 cards): Use cardstock or have professionally printed

Tip: To save as PDF, use Print → Save as PDF
